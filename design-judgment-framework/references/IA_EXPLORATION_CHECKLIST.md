# IA exploration checklist for agents and reviewers

Use before visual wireframes. Mark each item `evidenced`, `inferred`, `unknown`, or `not applicable`.

1. Are the primary user **jobs and entry points** explicit (not merely job titles)?
2. Are content/entity types and **relationships** modeled independently from navigation?
3. Have 3 genuinely different organization systems been explored for a new IA?
4. Are labels, default entry, search/filter scope and cross-links described for each?
5. Are the **same user tasks** walked through every candidate, including failure cases?
6. Are multi-persona conflicts, permissions, accessibility and scaling considered?
7. Are organization or task preferences visibly distinct from empirical evidence?
8. Are option ratings supported by reasoning and marked as estimates when untested?
9. Is the chosen primary organizational model explicit, even in a hybrid?
10. Are trade-offs, sensitivity and conditions to change the choice documented?
11. Is IA tested with appropriate methods before assuming a screen design works?
12. Can a reviewer trace final navigation and wireframes back to the chosen model?

## Acceptance threshold

For an open IA decision: require all 12 checks to be addressed (some may remain unknown but must be flagged). For a local UI refinement: require a documented IA skip reason and check that existing objects, hierarchy, labels and navigation are unaffected.
